About Us

First Step Careers supports young people in taking their first step into employment by helping them build confidence, create strong CVs, prepare for interviews, and understand real career pathways. The aim is to make the transition from education into the workplace less intimidating and more accessible for young people who may not always know where to begin.

Who We Support

First Step Careers works with young people and adults who may face additional barriers when entering the workforce.

We support individuals with disabilities, chronic illnesses, neurodivergence, and other vulnerabilities who may need extra guidance when navigating employment, job applications, and workplace expectations.

Our aim is to make career support more accessible, supportive, and realistic for people who may not always feel represented in traditional career advice.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make career support more accessible and inclusive. First Step Careers helps young people and adults — including those with disabilities, chronic illness, neurodivergence, or other barriers — build confidence, develop employability skills, and navigate realistic pathways into sustainable employment.
